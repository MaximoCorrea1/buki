Book Catcher

Setup — one field, once.

Why this is needed. Reading a book cover from a photo is the one thing Book Catcher can't do on your machine. It sends the cover and the post's text to a vision model, which names the book. Google's free tier covers normal use, and the key stays on this computer.

Get one at aistudio.google.com/apikey — it takes about two minutes and needs no billing details.

Stored locally, sent only to the provider below. Leave it empty and covers stay unreadable — links and post text still work.

Use a different provider

Any OpenAI-compatible chat endpoint works — Gemini, Cloudflare Workers AI, OpenRouter, or your own proxy. A proxy that holds its own credential lets you leave the key field blank.
