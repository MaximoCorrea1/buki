Setup: one field, once.

Why this is needed. Reading a book cover from a photo is the one thing Buki can't do on your machine. It sends the cover and the post's text to a vision model, which names the book. Google's free tier covers normal use. Your key is stored on this computer and sent only to that provider.

Get one at aistudio.google.com/apikey it takes about two minutes and needs no billing details.

Stored locally, sent only to the provider below. Leave it empty and Buki still catches books from retailer links and from a post's own words. Only reading a cover from a photo needs a key.

Buki earns a small commission if you buy through these links, at no extra cost to you. They only ever appear on books you have already saved.

Use a different provider

Any OpenAI-compatible chat endpoint works Gemini, Cloudflare Workers AI, OpenRouter, or your own proxy. A proxy that holds its own credential lets you leave the key field blank.

Recognition log

Buki keeps the last 200 recognition attempts on this computer, so the shelf can tell you how often it got the book right. It is never sent anywhere.
