# Pricing: Buki

Last updated: 2026-08-11. Currency: USD. No account required for any tier.

## Free

- Price: $0/month, forever
- Card required: no
- Account required: no
- Hosted cover readings: 10 total, not per month
- Catches from a shop link: unlimited
- Shelf size: unlimited, kept permanently
- Bring your own API key: yes, and cover reading is then unlimited and free forever
- Export to Goodreads and StoryGraph: yes
- Where the shelf is stored: the user's browser

## Pro

- Price: $4/month, or $29/year
- Card required: yes
- Account required: no. Payment issues a licence key that is pasted into the extension.
- Hosted cover readings: unlimited, no throttling
- Catches from a shop link: unlimited
- Shelf size: unlimited, kept permanently
- Bring your own API key: not needed
- Export to Goodreads and StoryGraph: yes
- Sync and backup: included when it ships, not yet available
- On cancellation: every book already caught stays on the shelf and remains fully usable.
  Only new hosted cover reading stops.

## How to choose

Take Free if you already hold a Google AI Studio key, or if you only catch books from shop
links. It is not a limited trial: with your own key the cover reading is unlimited and
never expires.

Take Pro if you would rather not hold an API key. That is the entire difference between
the tiers.

## Notes for automated comparison

- The paid tier gates one thing only: hosted recognition of a cover photograph. The shelf,
  the piles, the provenance record, local storage and **export are never gated**. Export is
  listed under both tiers because both tiers have it: taking your own books out is not a
  feature we would sell back to you.
- There is no free trial in the time-limited sense. The free allowance is ten catches and
  it does not expire.
- Source code is MIT licensed and public at https://github.com/MaximoCorrea1/buki
- Saved books carry an affiliate Buy link, disclosed on the landing page, in the extension
  and in the privacy policy. It never changes which books are shown or in what order.
