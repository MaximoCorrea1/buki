Buki — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-29

Buki keeps your reading list on your own computer. There is no Buki account, no Buki server, and no analytics.

What stays on your computer

  • Your shelf — every book you save, its intent, and the link to the post you found it in. Stored in your browser's local extension storage and never transmitted.
  • The recognition log — the most recent 200 attempts, so the shelf can show you how often it identified a book correctly. Never transmitted. Clear it any time from the options page.
  • Your API key, if you add one. Sent only to the recognition provider you configured.

What leaves your computer, and where it goes

  • To your recognition provider (Google Gemini by default, or any endpoint you configure): the image you asked Buki to read, and the text of the post it appeared in. Sent only when you press the book button or use the right-click menu — never in the background, and never for posts you did not act on.
  • To openlibrary.org: the recognized title and author, as a search query, to identify the book and fetch its cover. OpenLibrary is a project of the Internet Archive.

Nothing else is sent anywhere. Buki does not read your timeline, your account, your messages, or any page you have not explicitly asked it to act on.

Affiliate links

The Buy link on a saved book is an affiliate link. If you buy through it, Buki may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you.

  • The link appears only on books you have already chosen to save.
  • It never changes which books Buki shows you, or in what order.
  • Nothing about you is sent when the shelf loads — only when you choose to click.
  • You can pick between Amazon and Bookshop.org in the options page.

Removing your data

Uninstalling Buki deletes everything it stored. The recognition log can be cleared separately from the options page at any time.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and the new version ships with the extension update that requires it.
